## 2. How to join the test (send to each tester)

1. **Use the Google account you gave me** on your Android phone (Play Store, top right, your profile picture).
2. **Open the test link on your phone:** [OPT-IN LINK]
3. Tap **Become a tester** (or **Join**). Wait a moment for it to confirm.
4. Tap **Download it on Google Play** (or open the Play Store and search for Blackout IQ). Tap **Install**.
   **If the download does not start, or the page is blank:** search Google for **com.twelvescentspub.blackoutiq** (or open the Play Store app and search for that name), and open the Google Play result on your phone. Make sure the Play Store is signed in with the same Google account you gave me. A brand-new app can take a little while to appear, so if it still does not show, wait an hour and try again.
5. Open the app. The first launch shows a short tour. Allow notifications if asked: they only show download progress.

**What to try over the 14 days**
- Home: open the tiles. Try Survival Kit Checklist and Step By Step Problem Solver.
- Library: download one or two manuals and open them with no signal (Airplane Mode).
- Maps: download a small area around you, then use it offline.
- Family: add an emergency contact. Try the Documents Vault (it uses your screen lock or fingerprint).
- Videos: browse offline. Watching needs internet and opens YouTube.
- My Documents (in Library): add one of your own PDFs and search for it.

**Notes**
- The app is free and has no sign-in. Nothing you enter leaves your phone.
- Library and map downloads can be large. Use Wi-Fi.
- It is reference material, not medical or professional advice, and it cannot call emergency services.
- **Please stay opted in until I tell you the test is finished.** Leaving early can stop the whole test.

**Send me feedback:** reply to [SIGN-UP FORM OR EMAIL] with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps.
